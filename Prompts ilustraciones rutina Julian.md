# Gym exercise illustration prompts

This guide covers the 48 exercises in the three-day routine. The number beside each exercise is the number of separate illustrations to generate. Each prompt below describes one illustration. Use the same visual treatment for every prompt: a clear, anatomically believable adult athlete in a strict technical fitness illustration, fine cross-hatching on white paper, isolated with no background clutter, no labels, no lettering, no watermark, and equipment drawn accurately. Keep the athlete's outfit simple and unbranded. Show the whole body and all relevant equipment unless the pose requires a closer view.

## Day 1

### Mobility

#### Movilidad de cadera 90/90 — 2 illustrations

1. **Initial position:** The athlete sits upright on the floor with one leg folded in front at roughly 90 degrees and the other folded out to the side behind the hip, both knees bent. Keep the feet relaxed, hips grounded, chest lifted, and hands lightly resting on the floor for balance. Show the classic 90/90 hip-switch starting position from a clear three-quarter view.
2. **End position:** Show the athlete after rotating both knees together across the body to the opposite 90/90 position. The opposite leg is now folded in front and the other behind, with the pelvis turning under control and the torso tall. Keep both sitting bones as grounded as mobility allows.

#### Gato contento / Gato enojado — 2 illustrations

1. **Cat pose:** The athlete is on hands and knees, wrists under shoulders and knees under hips. Round the spine upward toward the ceiling, tuck the chin gently toward the chest, draw the abdomen in, and let the pelvis tuck under. Show the back forming a smooth dome.
2. **Cow pose:** From the same stable tabletop position, gently arch the back, tip the pelvis forward, lift the chest and tailbone, and look slightly ahead without cranking the neck. Keep the shoulders away from the ears and arms straight but not locked.

#### Dislocaciones de hombro con banda/bastón — 2 illustrations

1. **Starting position:** The athlete stands tall holding a light resistance band or straight dowel with both hands in a very wide overhand grip. Arms are extended in front at shoulder height, elbows straight but relaxed, shoulders down, ribs stacked over pelvis. Show the band lightly tensioned or the dowel horizontal.
2. **Overhead position:** Show the athlete holding the same wide grip with the arms raised overhead and slightly behind the head within a comfortable range. Keep elbows straight, shoulders controlled, ribs down, and torso upright; make the movement look like a shoulder mobility pass-through, not a forced backbend.

### Core

#### Plancha tradicional — 1 illustration

1. **Held position:** The athlete holds a straight-arm plank on hands and toes, hands directly under shoulders. The body forms one long line from head through heels; abdomen and glutes are braced, legs straight, and neck neutral with eyes toward the floor. Do not let the hips sag or rise.

#### Elevación de piernas en barra — 2 illustrations

1. **Hanging start:** The athlete hangs from a sturdy pull-up bar with an overhand shoulder-width grip, arms straight, shoulders engaged rather than shrugged, legs together and hanging vertically. Keep the torso still and show a controlled dead hang.
2. **Raised-leg position:** From the same hang, the athlete has lifted both straight legs together in front until the feet reach about hip height or higher, using abdominal control. Avoid swinging; keep the shoulders stable and the torso nearly vertical.

#### Mountain climbers — 2 illustrations

1. **Starting position:** The athlete is in a high plank with hands under shoulders, arms straight, head and spine aligned, and legs extended behind on the toes. Brace the trunk so the hips stay level.
2. **Knee-drive position:** Show one knee driven forward toward the chest while the other leg stays extended behind on its toes. Hands remain planted, shoulders stacked over wrists, and hips low and steady. Indicate a controlled alternating exercise without motion blur.

### Main block

#### Press banca con barra — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete lies on a flat bench with feet planted firmly, shoulder blades gently retracted, and a barbell held above the mid-chest in a slightly wider-than-shoulder overhand grip. Elbows are bent and angled moderately below the bar as it touches or nearly touches the lower chest. Wrists stay straight over elbows.
2. **Press position:** Show the barbell pressed upward over the mid-chest until the elbows are extended but not harshly locked. Keep the wrists stacked, shoulder blades supported on the bench, feet planted, and back in a natural small arch.

#### Remo con barra — 2 illustrations

1. **Hinged start:** The athlete stands with feet about hip-width, holding a barbell with an overhand grip just outside the legs. Hinge at the hips with a flat neutral back, knees softly bent, torso inclined forward, arms hanging straight beneath the shoulders.
2. **Rowed position:** Keep the same hip hinge and neutral spine as the athlete pulls the bar toward the lower ribs or upper abdomen. Elbows travel behind the torso, shoulder blades draw together, and the bar stays close to the body; do not shrug or round the back.

#### Press militar con mancuernas — 2 illustrations

1. **Start position:** The athlete stands upright with feet about hip-width, holding one dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height. Palms face forward, elbows bent and slightly below the wrists, forearms nearly vertical, abdomen braced, and ribs stacked over the pelvis.
2. **Overhead position:** Show both dumbbells pressed overhead, arms nearly straight and aligned beside the ears. Palms remain facing forward, wrists stacked above elbows, shoulders controlled, and torso upright without leaning back.

#### Curl con barra Z — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete stands tall holding an EZ-curl bar with a comfortable underhand grip on the angled sections. Arms hang by the sides with elbows close to the torso, wrists straight, shoulders relaxed, and knees soft.
2. **Curled position:** Show the bar lifted toward the upper abdomen or lower chest by bending the elbows. Keep elbows pinned near the sides, upper arms still, wrists neutral, and torso motionless; do not swing the body.

#### Extensión de tríceps en polea con cuerda — 2 illustrations

1. **Top position:** The athlete stands facing a high cable pulley, holding one end of the rope in each hand near the upper chest. Elbows are bent and tucked close to the ribs; forearms point upward toward the pulley. Stand tall with a slight forward lean and braced abdomen.
2. **Extended position:** Show the athlete pressing the rope down until the arms are straight beside the torso, then separating the rope ends slightly near the thighs. Keep elbows fixed by the sides, shoulders relaxed, wrists neutral, and torso still.

#### Sentadilla con barra — 2 illustrations

1. **Standing start:** The athlete stands with a barbell secured across the upper back, hands gripping the bar evenly, feet around shoulder-width and toes slightly turned out. Chest lifted, abdomen braced, spine neutral, and gaze forward.
2. **Bottom position:** Show a controlled squat with hips lowered between the thighs, knees tracking in the same direction as the toes, heels flat, and torso inclined naturally while the back remains neutral. Keep the bar balanced over the mid-foot.

#### Peso muerto rumano — 2 illustrations

1. **Standing start:** The athlete stands tall holding a barbell in front of the thighs with an overhand shoulder-width grip. Feet hip-width, knees softly bent, shoulders set, arms straight, abdomen braced, and spine neutral.
2. **Hinge position:** Show the athlete pushing the hips backward as the bar slides close down the thighs toward mid-shin. Knees remain only slightly bent, shins nearly vertical, back flat, neck aligned with the spine, and hamstrings visibly lengthened.

#### Prensa inclinada — 2 illustrations

1. **Loaded start:** The athlete sits in an inclined leg-press machine with the back and hips supported against the pad, feet about shoulder-width on the platform, toes slightly outward. Knees are bent, hands hold the seat handles, and the sled is controlled near the body.
2. **Pushed position:** Show the athlete pressing the platform away until the legs are nearly straight, stopping short of locking the knees. Keep feet flat on the platform, knees aligned with toes, hips and back against the pads, and show the sled moving along its rails.

#### Curl femoral sentado — 2 illustrations

1. **Bent-knee start:** The athlete sits upright in a seated leg-curl machine with the back against the pad, thighs secured under the upper restraint, and lower legs positioned in front of the padded ankle roller. Knees are bent and feet relaxed.
2. **Curled position:** Show the athlete pulling the ankle roller down and back beneath the seat by bending the knees. Keep thighs pressed under the restraint, hips seated, back supported, and movement controlled.

#### Elevación de talones de pie — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete stands upright on a calf-raise platform with the balls of the feet supported on the edge and heels lowered below it. Hold the machine handles or a stable support, knees straight but not locked, torso tall.
2. **Raised position:** Show the athlete rising onto the balls of both feet as high as possible, lifting the heels. Keep legs straight, ankles aligned, and body upright; show a brief controlled peak contraction.

## Day 2

### Mobility

#### Apertura torácica en cuadrupedia — 2 illustrations

1. **Closed position:** The athlete is on hands and knees, then places one hand lightly behind the head with the elbow pointing toward the floor. Hips stay square over the knees, supporting arm under shoulder, spine neutral.
2. **Open position:** Show the athlete rotating the chest toward the raised elbow, opening the upper back and turning the elbow toward the ceiling. Keep hips facing the floor and the supporting hand planted; rotation comes from the thoracic spine, not a twist of the lower back.

#### Estocadas con rotación — 2 illustrations

1. **Lunge setup:** The athlete stands tall and steps into a long forward lunge, front knee bent over the foot and rear leg extended with heel lifted. Hands are together in front of the chest; torso upright and hips facing forward.
2. **Rotated lunge:** Keep the lunge stable and rotate the torso toward the front leg, extending the arms gently in that direction. The pelvis remains steady, front knee tracks over toes, and the rear leg stays long. This is a controlled mobility reach, not a fast twist.

#### Circunducciones de tobillo y rodilla — 2 illustrations

1. **Ankle-circle position:** The athlete balances upright on one leg while the other foot is lifted slightly off the floor. One hand may lightly touch a wall or rail for balance. Draw a clear circular path implied by the raised foot's ankle rotation; support leg remains stable.
2. **Knee-circle position:** Show the athlete standing with feet close and knees softly bent, hands resting lightly above the knees. The knees are moving together through a small controlled circle while the feet remain planted and the torso stays upright.

### Core

#### Plancha lateral (ambos lados) — 2 illustrations

1. **Left-side hold:** The athlete lies on the left side and supports the body on the left forearm, elbow directly below shoulder. Legs are straight and stacked, feet aligned, hips lifted so the body forms a straight line from head to heels. Right hand rests on the hip; face looks forward.
2. **Right-side hold:** Mirror the position on the right side: right forearm supports the body with elbow below shoulder, legs stacked and straight, hips lifted, and left hand resting on the hip. Show a stable side plank with a neutral neck.

#### Crunch inverso — 2 illustrations

1. **Starting position:** The athlete lies on the back on a mat, arms resting beside the body or hands lightly holding the mat for stability. Hips stay on the floor, knees bent and brought above the hips, shins parallel to the floor.
2. **Curl position:** Show the athlete curling the pelvis and tailbone slightly off the mat, drawing the knees toward the chest using the abdominals. The movement is small and controlled; head and shoulders remain relaxed on the floor, with no swinging legs.

#### Russian twists (Giros rusos) — 2 illustrations

1. **Centered seated position:** The athlete sits on the floor with knees bent, heels resting lightly on the ground, torso leaned back slightly with a straight spine. Hold a small medicine ball or clasped hands close to the chest; abdomen braced.
2. **Rotated position:** Show the athlete rotating the ribcage to one side and carrying the hands or ball beside the hip. Keep the pelvis steady, spine long, and movement controlled. Legs remain in the same position; depict a rotation through the torso rather than flinging the arms.

### Main block

#### Tirón al pecho — 2 illustrations

1. **Overhead start:** The athlete sits at a lat-pulldown station with thighs secured beneath the pads, torso slightly inclined back, and hands holding the wide bar in an overhand grip. Arms are extended overhead, shoulders engaged, chest lifted.
2. **Pulldown position:** Show the athlete pulling the bar down in front of the face toward the upper chest. Elbows travel down and slightly back, shoulder blades draw down, and torso angle stays nearly unchanged. Keep the bar in front of the body, never behind the neck.

#### Press inclinado con mancuernas — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete lies on an incline bench with feet planted, back supported, and one dumbbell in each hand at upper-chest level. Palms face forward, elbows bent and slightly below the bench line, forearms vertical.
2. **Pressed position:** Show both dumbbells pressed above the upper chest until the arms are nearly straight, weights close but not touching. Wrists stay over elbows; shoulders remain supported and the back keeps a natural, stable position.

#### Elevaciones laterales para hombros — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete stands tall holding a light dumbbell at each side, palms facing inward, elbows softly bent, shoulders relaxed, feet hip-width, and abdomen braced.
2. **Raised position:** Show both arms lifted out to the sides to about shoulder height, elbows still softly bent and wrists neutral. Hands and elbows stay level, shoulders do not shrug, and torso remains still without swinging.

#### Curl martillo — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete stands upright holding a dumbbell in each hand at the sides with a neutral grip, palms facing inward toward the thighs. Elbows stay close to the torso, shoulders relaxed, wrists straight.
2. **Curled position:** Show both dumbbells lifted toward the shoulders by bending the elbows while maintaining the neutral grip, palms still facing each other. Upper arms remain still, wrists neutral, and body does not lean or swing.

#### Press francés con mancuernas — 2 illustrations

1. **Bent-elbow position:** The athlete lies on a flat bench holding a dumbbell in each hand above the shoulders with palms facing each other. Upper arms point mostly upward and elbows are bent so the dumbbells sit beside the head; shoulders stay steady.
2. **Extended position:** Show the athlete straightening both elbows to raise the dumbbells above the shoulders. Keep upper arms nearly vertical and still, wrists neutral, and the weights controlled without banging together.

#### Peso muerto convencional — 2 illustrations

1. **Floor setup:** The athlete stands with mid-foot beneath a barbell, feet about hip-width. Hinge and bend the knees to grip the bar just outside the legs, arms straight, back flat, chest lifted, hips set between shoulders and knees, gaze slightly forward and down.
2. **Lockout position:** Show the athlete standing tall with the bar close against the thighs, hips and knees extended, shoulders set, and arms straight. Spine is neutral, abdomen braced, and the athlete does not lean backward.

#### Sentadilla búlgara — 2 illustrations

1. **Split-stance setup:** The athlete stands in a long split stance with the rear foot supported on a low bench behind, laces down. Front foot is planted far enough forward for a stable squat; torso upright, hips square, hands on hips or holding dumbbells at the sides.
2. **Lowered position:** Show the athlete bending the front knee and lowering the back knee toward the floor while keeping the front heel grounded and knee aligned over the foot. Rear foot remains supported, pelvis faces forward, and torso stays controlled. Indicate that the setup is mirrored for the other leg.

#### Extensión de cuádriceps en máquina — 2 illustrations

1. **Bent-knee start:** The athlete sits against the backrest of a leg-extension machine, knees bent at about 90 degrees, shins behind the padded roller near the ankles. Hands hold the side handles; thighs remain supported and feet relaxed.
2. **Extended position:** Show the athlete straightening both knees to lift the padded roller until the legs are almost horizontal. Keep hips and back against the seat, toes neutral, and stop short of forcefully locking the knees.

#### Hip thrust — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete sits on the floor with the upper back resting against a sturdy bench, knees bent and feet flat about hip-width apart. A padded barbell rests across the crease of the hips; hands steady the bar. Hips are lowered, chin slightly tucked, spine neutral.
2. **Top position:** Show the athlete driving through the feet to lift the hips until the torso is nearly horizontal from shoulders to knees. Shins are close to vertical, knees aligned over feet, glutes contracted, ribs down, and lower back not overarched.

#### Elevación de talones sentado — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete sits in a seated calf-raise machine, knees bent about 90 degrees beneath the padded thigh restraint. Balls of the feet rest on the platform edge and heels are lowered. Back upright, hands holding the handles.
2. **Raised position:** Show the athlete pressing through the balls of both feet to lift the heels high. Knees remain under the pads and torso remains still; emphasize ankle movement and a controlled peak.

## Day 3

### Mobility

#### Sentadilla profunda isométrica con empuje de codos — 2 illustrations

1. **Standing setup:** The athlete stands with feet a little wider than shoulder-width and toes turned out slightly. Arms relaxed in front, spine tall, and weight balanced evenly over both feet, preparing to descend.
2. **Deep squat hold:** Show the athlete in a deep, comfortable squat with heels grounded, knees tracking over the toes, and torso upright. Elbows press gently outward against the inner knees while palms meet at the chest. Keep the back long and the position relaxed rather than strained.

#### Paso de valla dinámico (talón-punta) — 2 illustrations

1. **Raised-leg position:** The athlete stands tall beside an imaginary low hurdle, balancing on one leg while lifting the other knee and guiding that leg outward and over the hurdle. Ankle is flexed so the heel leads, toes drawn upward; torso remains upright.
2. **Step-through position:** Show the lifted leg completing the arc and stepping down heel-first on the other side of the imaginary hurdle, then rolling toward the forefoot. Keep the movement controlled, hips level, and support leg stable. Indicate that the athlete repeats on the other leg.

#### Y-T-W extensiones escapulares prone — 3 illustrations

1. **Y position:** The athlete lies face down on a mat with forehead hovering or resting lightly, legs long and relaxed. Arms extend overhead diagonally into a Y, thumbs pointing upward; lift the hands slightly by drawing the shoulder blades down and together, without arching the lower back.
2. **T position:** Show the athlete prone with arms stretched straight out to the sides in a T, thumbs up. Lift the hands a small distance using the upper-back muscles, neck long and shoulders away from ears.
3. **W position:** Show the athlete prone with elbows bent and pulled down beside the ribcage, forming a W shape with the arms. Hands hover near shoulder level, thumbs up, shoulder blades gently drawn together, and lower back relaxed.

### Core

#### Rodillo abdominal (Ab wheel rollout) — 2 illustrations

1. **Kneeling start:** The athlete kneels on a mat with knees hip-width apart, hands gripping both handles of an ab wheel placed on the floor beneath the shoulders. Arms are straight, back neutral, glutes lightly braced, and gaze down.
2. **Extended position:** Show the athlete rolling the wheel forward as the arms reach ahead and the torso lowers toward a long diagonal line from knees through hips to shoulders. Keep the abdomen braced, hips from sagging, and spine neutral; show a controlled range that the athlete can return from.

#### Hollow hold (Mantención en canoa) — 1 illustration

1. **Held position:** The athlete lies on the back and curls the shoulders slightly off the mat, chin tucked gently. Arms extend overhead beside the ears and straight legs hover low above the floor, with the lower back pressed into the mat. Show a tight curved hollow-body shape held still; knees may be slightly bent if needed to keep the back grounded.

#### Bird dog (Alternado controlado) — 2 illustrations

1. **Tabletop start:** The athlete is on hands and knees with wrists below shoulders and knees below hips. Back is flat, abdomen braced, neck aligned with spine, and gaze down.
2. **Reach position:** Show the athlete extending one arm forward and the opposite leg backward until both are parallel to the floor. Hips remain square to the ground, trunk steady, supporting arm straight, and neck neutral. Indicate alternating sides by showing a controlled, balanced pose rather than motion blur.

### Main block

#### Press Arnold — 2 illustrations

1. **Initial pose:** The arms are up in a tight defensive boxing-guard stance, elbows tucked in front of the chest and pointing forward. In each hand, the athlete holds a horizontal dumbbell at chin level. The hands are fully reversed, with palms facing inward toward the face. Forearms are vertical, back straight, feet stable, and abdomen braced.
2. **Final pose:** Show the Arnold press at full overhead extension. Both arms are extended upward beside the ears, holding horizontal dumbbells at the highest point. The grip is fully pronated, with palms facing forward away from the face. Keep wrists stacked over elbows, ribs down, and spine straight without leaning backward.

#### Remo en polea baja (Grip neutro) — 2 illustrations

1. **Extended start:** The athlete sits at a low cable row station with feet braced on the footplates, knees slightly bent, and a close neutral-grip handle held with palms facing each other. Arms reach forward, torso upright with a small hip hinge, back neutral, shoulders controlled.
2. **Rowed position:** Show the handle pulled toward the lower ribs or navel as elbows travel back close to the body. Chest stays lifted, torso remains steady, shoulder blades draw together, and wrists stay straight; do not lean far backward.

#### Cruces en polea alta (Pecho) — 2 illustrations

1. **Open position:** The athlete stands centered between two high cable pulleys, one handle in each hand. Arms are open out and slightly behind the torso with elbows softly bent, feet staggered for balance, chest lifted, and torso leaning forward slightly.
2. **Crossed position:** Show the athlete bringing both hands down and inward in a wide arc until they meet or cross slightly in front of the lower chest. Keep elbows softly bent, shoulders down, torso stable, and cables taut. The motion comes from the chest and shoulders, not a body swing.

#### Curl concentrado unilateral — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete sits on a bench with feet wide and torso hinged forward slightly. One elbow rests against the inside of the same-side thigh, arm nearly straight toward the floor, holding a dumbbell with an underhand grip. The free hand rests on the other thigh; back remains long.
2. **Curled position:** Show the athlete bending the working elbow to bring the dumbbell toward the shoulder while the upper arm stays braced against the inner thigh. Wrist remains straight, torso still, and shoulder relaxed. The other arm remains supported; mirror the pose for the opposite arm.

#### Fondos en banco/cajon — 2 illustrations

1. **Top position:** The athlete sits at the edge of a sturdy bench or box, hands gripping the edge beside the hips with fingers facing forward. Slide the hips just off the edge, elbows straight, shoulders down, and legs extended forward with heels on the floor.
2. **Lowered position:** Show the athlete lowering the hips in front of the bench by bending the elbows backward, keeping the back close to the edge and shoulders controlled. Stop around a comfortable elbow bend; feet remain planted and the chest stays open.

#### Sentadilla Goblet pesada — 2 illustrations

1. **Standing start:** The athlete stands with feet about shoulder-width and toes slightly out, holding a heavy dumbbell vertically against the chest by one end with both hands. Elbows point down, chest lifted, spine neutral, and abdomen braced.
2. **Bottom position:** Show a deep controlled squat with hips between the thighs, knees tracking over the toes, heels grounded, and elbows inside the knees without forcing them outward. Keep the dumbbell close to the chest and torso upright as mobility allows.

#### Estocadas caminando — 2 illustrations

1. **Step position:** The athlete holds a dumbbell in each hand at the sides and steps forward into a long walking lunge. Front foot is planted, rear heel lifted, torso tall, hips facing forward, and feet remain on two separate tracks for balance.
2. **Lowered stride:** Show the athlete lowering until both knees bend, with the rear knee hovering just above the floor and the front knee aligned over the ankle. Dumbbells hang still at the sides, chest upright, and front heel grounded. Indicate that the next step alternates legs.

#### Curl femoral — 2 illustrations

1. **Extended position:** The athlete lies face down on a prone leg-curl machine, hips and torso supported on the pads, legs nearly straight, and ankles placed beneath the roller. Hands hold the machine grips; pelvis stays down.
2. **Curled position:** Show the athlete bending both knees to draw the ankle roller toward the back of the thighs. Hips remain pressed to the pad, knees stay aligned with the machine pivot, and the motion is smooth without lifting the pelvis.

#### Puente de glúteos — 2 illustrations

1. **Lowered position:** The athlete lies on the back on a mat with knees bent, feet flat about hip-width apart, and arms resting by the sides. Hips rest on the floor, lower back neutral, heels close enough that fingertips could reach them.
2. **Raised position:** Show the athlete pressing through both feet to lift the hips until shoulders, hips, and knees form a diagonal line. Knees remain aligned over feet, glutes engaged, ribs down, and neck relaxed on the floor.

#### Step-up con mancuernas — 2 illustrations

1. **Ready position:** The athlete stands facing a sturdy knee-height box or step, holding a dumbbell in each hand at the sides. One whole foot is planted flat on top of the step, torso upright, hips square, and supporting leg ready to drive upward.
2. **Top position:** Show the athlete standing tall on the step with the lead leg extended and the trailing foot lifted just above the surface or lightly placed beside it. Dumbbells hang steady, knee tracks over toes, hips level, and the athlete does not push excessively off the trailing foot. Indicate that the movement is repeated with the other leg leading.
